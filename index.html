How to actually use this

Reading is not preparation. Reading builds recognition; the exam tests cold execution at six minutes a question with no calculator. Those are different skills.

So: skim the guide for the shape, then take Simulation A cold and timed. Whatever it exposes, fix in the guide, then drill that chapter. Repeat with Simulation B. Two-thirds of your time should be problems, not prose.

Practice questions are original, written to match the released final's skill mix. They are not past Purdue questions, and no exam predicts another.
